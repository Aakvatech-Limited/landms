If the check fails

  1. Make sure the code was scanned or typed exactly as printed.
  2. Compare every detail on the screen with the paper. Any difference means the paper was altered.
  3. A receipt shown as Cancelled was issued by GVA but no longer stands. A receipt shown as Not valid is unknown to GVA's records.
  4. If the receipt is not confirmed, do not rely on it. Call our office on +255 22 240 0646 or write to info@gva.co.tz, quoting the receipt number and code.
